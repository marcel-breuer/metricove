# Code of Conduct

Metricove welcomes constructive contributions from people of all backgrounds. Be respectful in reviews, issues and discussions, assume good intentions, and provide actionable feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing and sharing another person's confidential information are not acceptable. Maintainers may moderate comments, close discussions or restrict participation for violations.

Report concerns privately via repository maintainers' GitHub contact options. Please do not post private information in public issues. This policy may be expanded through a community-reviewed proposal.
